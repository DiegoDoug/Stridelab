# Incident Flow

1. D05 owns incident coordination unless security incident policy delegates to D04.
2. Classify severity and affected users/data.
3. Stabilize and preserve evidence.
4. Engage D04 immediately for security/privacy/safety incidents.
5. Mitigate or rollback under approved emergency policy.
6. Verify recovery.
7. Run incident review.
8. Create durable corrective actions and project-state updates.
